# The lamp lighter

> "Lead Lamp Lighter and Maintainer for the Path" (README)

Companion to OZ.md and HORIZONS.md.

## The job

A lamp lighter doesn't own the road and doesn't build the city. He walks the path at dusk so the next
traveller can see the bricks. The light is for whoever comes after.

In OZ.md terms: the Wizard asks to be trusted. The lamp lighter makes the road checkable. He's the outside
reader, kept as a job with a route instead of a one-off.

## Three lamps

| lamp | what it lights | examples |
|---|---|---|
| **the record** | what happened, who signed it, when | BikeChainz's sha256 pins; mapsync's signed roots; append-only logs; handover notes |
| **the check** | whether the record is still true | BikeChainz G1 (every green must go red under sabotage); `g1_review.py`; mapsync `verify` and `g1` |
| **the window** | what a person actually sees | deterministic output, so the same state shows the same bytes to everyone and a screenshot is an exact test |

## Rules

1. **Light the road, not yourself.** Nothing certifies itself. A lamp that only lights its own post is a Wizard.
2. **Walk the whole route.** A lit lamp at the city gate doesn't help someone still at the first brick.
3. **Mark the dark stretches.** ⊬ is an honest lamp. Calling a dark road lit is the one thing a lamp lighter
   must never do.
4. **Lay your own bricks where you can.** A road built from parts you never opened has a curtain in it.
   Own them, or name them.
5. **Leave it for the next one.** "May the things we build, be made in steel ... to protect the Motte and Moat
   we leave behind." Straw is the lighter; steel is the record that outlasts him.

## The needle's eye

"Location: Inside the Needles Eye." "The only way out is through."

The needle's eye is the narrow throat: the place where everything has to pass single file, and where one
check sees everything. In BLACKHOLES.md it's the throat of a bounce, where one universe pinches down before
opening into the next. In a trust system it's the one door everything must cross, which is the one place a
lamp does the most good.
